# Product Framing

Describe what this product is and why it exists. Agents and contributors read this first to
understand the product before they change it. Keep it short, in plain language, and up to date.
Put stable scope in `docs/evergreen/mvp.md` and design in
`docs/evergreen/architecture.md`.

## Problem
What problem does the product solve, and why does it need solving now?

## Users
Who uses the product, and what are they trying to get done?

## Vision
What does the product look like when it works well?

## Out of scope
What the product does not try to do.

## Principles
Rules of thumb for making decisions when the docs don't give an answer.
